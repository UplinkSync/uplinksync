## About

**UplinkSync** is a technology company based in Idaho, helping local businesses and homeowners
get more from their technology. We pair hands-on IT support with modern web, automation, and
aerial-imaging services so clients can work with a single, dependable partner instead of juggling
vendors.

Our approach is practical and outcome-focused: keep systems running, keep them secure, and put
automation to work so people spend less time on repetitive tasks and more time on what matters.

## 🛠️ Services

Area | What we do
--- | ---
**💻 Local IT Support &amp; Managed Services** | On-site and remote support, workstation &amp; network setup, monitoring, backups, and ongoing managed care for small businesses.
**🚁 Drone / Aerial Photography &amp; Inspection** | FAA Part 107–certified aerial photography, video, mapping, and structure &amp; roof inspection for real estate, construction, and property owners.
**⚙️ Business Automation** | Workflow automation, systems integration, and custom tooling that connects the apps a business already uses and removes manual busywork.
